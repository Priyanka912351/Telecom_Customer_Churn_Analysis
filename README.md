# Telecom_Customer_Churn_Analysis

Project Description

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior in a telecom company using SQL for data querying and Power BI for visualization. The aim was to identify key patterns, customer segments, and risk factors contributing to churn in order to propose effective retention strategies.
Using SQL, various metrics were extracted including total customers, churn rate, tenure, satisfaction score, average revenue, CLTV, and churn distribution by contract type, city, and service usage. Power BI dashboards were then built to visually interpret these insights and highlight business performance trends.

Problem Statement

Customer churn significantly affects telecom revenues and operational costs. Identifying which customers are at risk of leaving, and understanding why, helps companies implement better strategies to enhance retention and profitability.

This analysis aims to answer:
Which customer groups have the highest churn rate?
What factors (e.g., contract type, CLTV, monthly charges, satisfaction) are driving churn?
How can strategic changes reduce future churn?

Key Insights & Findings

Overall churn rate: Approximately 27% of customers have churned.
Contract impact: Month-to-month customers show higher churn rates compared to long-term contracts.
Satisfaction correlation: Customers with lower satisfaction scores and higher churn scores are more likely to leave.
Revenue loss: A noticeable revenue drop is linked to churned customers, emphasizing the need for retention programs.
Demographic trends: Certain cities and younger customers yielded higher churn rates, while loyal long-tenure customers generated higher CLTV.

Business Insights & Recommendations

Improve retention programs for month-to-month customers via loyalty rewards or discounts for contract upgrades.
Enhance customer experience by addressing service pain points identified through satisfaction scores.
Target marketing campaigns to high-risk demographics revealed by churn scoring.
Personalized offers for low-tenure and high-churn-score customers could significantly reduce churn.
Monitor key metrics (tenure, CLTV, churn rate by contract) in a dynamic Power BI dashboard for ongoing improvement.

Conclusion

By integrating SQL-based analytics and Power BI visualization, this project delivers actionable business intelligence for reducing telecom churn. The findings help the company shift from reactive churn management to a proactive, data-driven customer retention approach.
